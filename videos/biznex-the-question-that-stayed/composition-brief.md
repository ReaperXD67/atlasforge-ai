# Composition direction

Make the question physically disappear beneath needless decoration. The opening
already contains a moving ribbon; compliment and apology add separate layers with
distinct colors and shapes. The failed communication is visible before the turn.
On “So I tried again,” reverse the complexity: loosen the wraps, straighten the
message and cross the space between two abstract endpoints. Hold the clear question
long enough to read, with the restrained independent-community context beneath it.

Keep measured exact captions separate from environmental type. Favor clear
silhouettes, generous cream space, rich ink-green anchors, coral and brass ribbon
accents. Camera changes should reveal cause and meaning. No copied social interface.
Sound should support the wrap, failed send, unspooling and settled ending; preserve
the liked narrator and the quiet contrast in her complete thoughts.

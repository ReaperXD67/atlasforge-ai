Official Atomy U.S. membership registration guidance distinguishes Consumer Membership and Distributor Membership.

Consumer Membership is for people who want to purchase products for personal use. Consumer accounts are not eligible for commission.

Distributor Membership includes access to business tools and commission eligibility. Eligibility is not a promise of earnings.

Both flows include accepting terms, U.S. mobile verification, personal details, and selecting or confirming a sponsor. The Distributor flow additionally requests Social Security and bank information.

Applicants must be legal U.S. residents who are at least 18 years old.

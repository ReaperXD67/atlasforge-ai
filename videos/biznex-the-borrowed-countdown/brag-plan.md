# Shareable story plan

The proof is an actual physical change: a small decision token stops following the
giant timer and chooses a separate route. The hook is visible immediately and the
camera later reveals what the first view concealed.

Build tension with scale, mechanical cadence and increasingly tight framing.
Create a real silence at the turn. Let the final move and question settle long
enough to read. Do not finish on a sales screen or repeat the Yes Machine's gates.

Adapted creative planning from the pinned MIT BRAG skill; original script and
composition, not a recreation of someone else's film. One central subject, a clear
before/after choice, restrained headings, voice-timed movement and an honest payoff.

# Revive pitch design system

- Canvas: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 300 seconds.
- Palette: graphite `#17201d`, acid lime `#d8ff4f`, warm white `#f4f6f2`, proof blue
  `#dce9ff` where live-system evidence needs separation.
- Typography: Inter/Segoe UI-like sans for editorial copy; Consolas-like mono for APIs, hashes,
  policy labels, timestamps, and evidence chips.
- Evidence: product screenshots stay sharp inside one bordered viewport frame with browser chrome
  excluded. Push-ins are limited to 100–107%.
- Presenter: Nexa uses static transparent expression images only. Expression swaps are editorial
  punctuation; there is no lip movement or facial deformation.
- Motion: restrained 220–420 ms reveals, straight evidence-state changes, and a simple lime wipe
  between chapters. Captions hold complete thoughts for at least five seconds.
- Truth labels: dashboard and customer data are marked simulated; hosted API and storage proof are
  marked live. No visual implies official Razorpay endorsement or production merchant adoption.

## Title

This 'Online Job' Asks You to Pay First — Stop

## Description

An unexpected task job, a small initial payment, a growing app balance, then a demand to deposit your own money: the FTC warns that this is a task-scam pattern. The on-screen conversation is a fictional illustration, not an actual recruiter or earning record.

Do not send money to unlock earnings. Ignore suspicious unsolicited job messages and verify employers independently. There are no guaranteed replies, legitimate jobs or income from this video.

Official FTC sources, checked 10 October 2026:
https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/08/how-spot-avoid-task-scams
https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/04/job-offer-text-probably-scam

If you have lost money, contact your payment provider promptly and use your local official fraud-reporting channel.

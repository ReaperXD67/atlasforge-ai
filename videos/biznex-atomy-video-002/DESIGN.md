# BizNex animated editorial system

Concept angle: a premium animated field guide in which confusing business vocabulary
is physically sorted, weighed, and connected in front of the viewer.

Use warm black, ivory, and one unmistakable gold accent. Frames should resemble an
editorial documentary title sequence crossed with an approachable hand-built diagram,
not a dashboard or presentation deck. Large condensed display typography carries the
hook; Segoe UI carries explanations; Consolas labels sources and numeric units.

Nexa Toon is a credible adult guide, not a children's mascot. Keep her between 28% and
40% of the frame when present. Her lip-sync mouth is deliberately graphic and clean,
supported by blink, eyebrow, hand, and body drift so the performance does not feel like
a mouth pasted onto a still.

Each chapter keeps three layers: warm radial glow and faint topology lines in the
background; the explanatory object in the midground; source labels, registration marks,
and progress ticks in the foreground. Avoid generic card grids, neon gradients, tiny
web typography, financial hype, currency rain, and stock-office imagery.

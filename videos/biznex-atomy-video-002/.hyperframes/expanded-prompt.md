# BizNex Video 002 — expanded production direction

## Style block

Warm-black editorial animation using `#0B0A0A`, ivory `#FFF4DD`, muted sand
`#C8BDA7`, and BizNex gold `#F4B942`, with `#FF6B5E` reserved for caution and
`#75D69C` for verified/qualified states. League Gothic owns chapter statements,
Montserrat owns plain-language explanation, and IBM Plex Mono owns thresholds,
source labels, and units.

Rhythm: `HOOK — BUILD — CLARIFY — CLIMB — PEAK — QUESTION — RESOLVE — CTA`.
Inside each long chapter, a meaningful object enters or changes every 6–12 seconds.

## Global rules

- Background stays warm black but changes topology: doors, rails, token fields,
  ladder coordinates, pool contours, evidence columns, and the final map.
- Gold is the focal signal, never a generic glow wash.
- Chapter transitions primarily use a 0.4s editorial push; topic changes use a
  0.55s gold circle iris; the outro uses a 0.7s blur crossfade to black.
- Nexa appears in Frames 01, 04, and 08. Her performance combines Rhubarb mouth
  cues, deterministic blink moments, eyebrow emphasis, hand drift, and small body
  settles. No continuous bobbing or exaggerated cartoon bounce.
- Captions remain within the lower safe area, two lines maximum, with the current
  sentence in ivory and one important term in gold where useful.
- Sources appear as restrained monospace rails, never as tiny legal text.

## Per-scene expansion

### 01 — Two doors

Concept: make the viewer feel the presentation started in the middle, then give them
a clean entrance. Two doors divide the unknown; Nexa occupies the deciding seam.
Depth: faint gold topology behind; doors and headline in midground; AI HOST label,
chapter tick, and source posture in foreground. Choreography: doors PULL apart,
headline WATERFALLS, Nexa SLIDES and SETTLES, glow BLOOMS. Transition: editorial
push left, 0.4s, power3.in/out.

### 02 — Two-leg machine

Concept: the compensation structure behaves like a visible balancing machine.
Depth: topology grid; center node and rails; PV tokens, measuring bracket, labels.
Choreography: rails DRAW, nodes POP, tokens TRAVEL, bars GROW, bracket SNAPS to the
smaller side. Transition: gold circle iris from center node, 0.55s.

### 03 — PV language

Concept: PV is a measuring language, not a magical currency conversion. A threshold
physically assembles while a false dollar interpretation is rejected.
Choreography: tokens POP into a 10,000 stack, progress fill GROWS, caution seal
STAMPS once, source label TYPES ON. Transition: editorial push up, 0.4s.

### 04 — Class ladder

Concept: qualification is climbed rung by rung; a rank name is not a salary.
Choreography: ladder DRAWS, ticker CLICKS through five classes, thresholds COUNT and
LOCK, 44% counter GROWS, Nexa SLIDES into a small presenter aperture. Transition:
gold circle iris from the 44% figure, 0.55s.

### 05 — Allocation pools

Concept: three system buckets must never be read as one individual's paycheck.
Choreography: vessels FILL in sequence, numbers COUNT with their fills, bases LABEL,
then the frame FOLDS into split cards and the unequal sign STAMPS. Transition:
editorial push left, 0.4s.

### 06 — Structure versus prediction

Concept: celebrate what the chart can reveal while clearly showing what it cannot.
Choreography: cards PIVOT inward, mechanism labels POP, question marks ARRIVE from
three directions, checklist ticks LOCK. Transition: focus pull, 0.55s.

### 07 — Claim versus chart

Concept: physically separate evidence from mechanics, then rebuild the complete mental
model. Choreography: claim PULLS left, chart PULLS right, evidence rail DRAWS, six
model pieces EXPAND from center and LOCK around the map. Transition: gentle blur
crossfade, 0.6s.

### 08 — Next question

Concept: the viewer chooses tomorrow's lesson and sees the daily-series promise.
Choreography: Nexa SLIDES in, three options POP sequentially, comment arrow PULSES
once, BizNex lockup DRAWS and HOLDS. Final fade to black over 0.7s.

## Recurring motifs

Two gold rails, a smaller-side bracket, circular PV tokens, mono source labels, and an
eight-tick chapter rail repeat across the film so every visual change still belongs to
one system.

## Negative prompt

No stock-office footage, generic dashboard cards, blue-purple SaaS gradients, currency
rain, income promises, childish mascot behavior, endless breathing loops, tiny web text,
or decorative motion unrelated to the spoken point.

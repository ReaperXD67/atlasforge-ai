# The Question That Stayed

**I kept making my question sound nicer.** An original fictional miniature about
letting a clear question survive the urge to decorate it.

Original procedural ribbons wrap around a message, then unfold into a bridge
between two abstract endpoints. The film uses code-authored geometry, the local
Three.js/GSAP/HyperFrames stack, the approved synthetic narrator and original sound.
No image/video models or paid services are used.

Rendered and inspected **11 November 2026**: 25.333 seconds, 760 frames, twelve
shots, 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, H.264 and stereo 48 kHz AAC. The nine-stage run passed
in **462.375 seconds** using prepared narration. Full media decode,
five encoded-speech checks, exact captions and zero audio lag passed. All ten decoded
contact sheets (79 unique frames) were inspected with no blocking sampled finding.
Full-speed playback and subjective listening were not verified by these checks.

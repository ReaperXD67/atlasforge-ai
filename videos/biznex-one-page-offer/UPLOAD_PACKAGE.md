## Title

No Clients? Build This One-Page Offer First

## Description

Build a one-page offer before another round of vague outreach. We turn 'creative solutions for everyone' into a fictional café menu-page service with a customer, bounded deliverable, scope, honest sample, clear price basis, process and next step. The episode finishes with a fill-in template and a quick reader test.

This is an original BizNex educational framework, not a validated sales system. The business, samples, time estimates and pricing decisions are illustrative. No clients, testimonials, revenue or conversion results are claimed. Replies, clients and income are not guaranteed. A clear offer is only useful if the need exists and you can deliver the work.

General marketing-planning background: https://www.sba.gov/counseling/manage-your-business/

Use a simple document or a tool you already have; no paid software is required. Respect privacy, ownership and applicable outreach rules.

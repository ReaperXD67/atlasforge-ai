## Limits

This is 2.5D animation of generated artwork, not native generated-video footage. Relative depth cannot reconstruct hidden surfaces, and selective motion does not create a fully articulated creature performance. Individual vocal entrances remain model-derived and are not independently human-auditioned. Technical gates and sampled image review do not establish audience reaction or every-frame subjective quality.

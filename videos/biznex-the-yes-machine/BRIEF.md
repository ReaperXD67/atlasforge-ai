# The Yes Machine

A fictional, first-person miniature about the cost of an automatic yes. Make the audience feel the chain reaction, then the relief of choosing deliberately. This is the **next** BizNex film; The Second Question stays unchanged.

**LOOK:** tactile mechanical sculpture; ink navy `#081923`, warm cream `#eee1c8`, coral `#ef7059`, brass `#dfb16b`; a continuous 3D world, directional studio lighting, grounded shadows, carefully framed camera travel. These material colors come from `assets/world.json`; studio lighting changes their rendered appearance. Locally bundled Montserrat for readable editorial text. No image generation, downloaded stock montage, fake dashboard, generic particles or card slideshow.

**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Target roughly 30 seconds; exact duration follows measured narration and a readable ending. BRAG's usual 15–25 second product-launch format is adapted to this explicitly narrated social story, rather than rushing speech to meet its default.

**Hook:** a huge physical YES button under pressure. “My most expensive habit?” creates the question; the press answers it. The first frame must already contain a composed, meaningful image—no fade from black and no unrelated one-frame poster flash.

**Story:** press → chain reaction → escalating demands → pause → three practical questions → a deliberate choice. Every new view reveals a consequence or changes the audience's understanding. A prop's motion motivates the next shot. Let stillness be the strongest moment after acceleration.

**Voice:** preserve the existing original synthetic voice identity. Perform complete thoughts: amused confession, rueful acceleration, vulnerable hesitation, an actual pause, curious questions, warm relief. Native model directions and measured thought groups; no random pitch jitter or claim of human indistinguishability. `script.txt` is the canonical spoken text.

**Sound:** original deterministic score and physical mechanism sounds. Match accents to visible motion. Pull the bed down at “I paused”; leave the final line clear. Keep the voice intact, mix around it, and inspect the audio encoded in the actual final MP4.

**Context:** fictional illustrative story with synthetic narration, not a personal testimonial. Display that disclosure clearly. Atomy is educational context for informed product/business decisions; the end slate identifies BizNex as an independent community. Do not imply official affiliation, guaranteed income or product/health outcomes.

**Delivery:** reusable declared stages, final rendered MP4, captions, poster, upload copy, license/provenance, measured QA and portable source package. Source hashes and exact resume behavior must be tested. Creative quality is assessed from decoded frames and listening, not a fabricated “viral score.” No social upload is implied by this production run.

# Illustrated story treatment

She Almost Sent the Wrong Message

Warm human-scale rooms, a consistent original Mira/Asha pair, physically legible decisions and an ordinary noncommercial ending. The film labels fiction on every frame. 3 distinct world structures are used.

The phone, supplies, paper and calendar are active subjects. Camera reframing follows literal measured speech; no fabricated word timing, earnings proof, product imagery, testimonial or CTA. Registry message/typing geometry is adapted from installed chat-message and typing-indicator primitives.

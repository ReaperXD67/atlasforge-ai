## Title

Atomy Consumer vs Distributor: Pick the Right Membership #Shorts

## Description

Before you register with Atomy U.S., choose the membership path that matches why you are joining.

Consumer membership is for purchasing products for personal use. Distributor membership adds business tools and commission eligibility; eligibility is not a guarantee of earnings. Both paths include terms, U.S. mobile verification, member details, and a sponsor. The Distributor flow also asks for Social Security and bank information. U.S. applicants must be 18 or older.

Always verify the current process on Atomy's official U.S. pages:

- Registration guide: https://us.atomy.com/cst/userGuide/24100000001691
- Join page: https://us.atomy.com/join/memberGate
- Member agreement: https://us.atomy.com/common/footerTermsInfo?isHtml=Y&tabIndex=1

Save this before registering, and share it with someone choosing their membership path.

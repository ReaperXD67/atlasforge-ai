## Shared production direction

Make the editing tangible: paper doors unfold into the factual comparison; a cup reveals a missed question; an empty index-card space gets filled; two ribbons trace a note back to its source; a paper fold reveals total cost; three empty frames become a checklist. Use original props and deliberate camera moves. Hold the reveal long enough to read it; avoid a branded intro, incessant cuts or a sales crescendo.

Keep one licensed narrator identity. The embedded voice direction uses only supported emotion names, pace bounds and pauses. Kokoro can vary pace and pauses; its emotion labels are creative intent rather than verified emotional acting. OpenAI instructions or Chatterbox numeric controls are optional configured alternatives, with a listening check. Do not clone the reference creator’s voice or copy the reel’s footage or music.

All source graphics should be original summaries. The linked pages establish facts, not permission to reproduce site screenshots or brand artwork. Use project-owned or licensed sound, authorized performers/voices, and provenance records for each asset. Show no filled personal-ID form, bank details or private message. Confirm and disclose actual commercial relationships before release.

## 1. Two Signup Paths. One Real Question.

**question-first · membership-choice**

A fictional adult folds two paper doors, then writes what she actually came to do on a tiny shopping list.

**Exact narration**

> Which door matches why you came here? A fictional scene. Mira folds two paper doors, then notices her tiny shopping list. Atomy’s U.S. guide separates consumer and distributor registration. The distributor path includes Social Security and bank information. She puts the list between the doors. First name your purpose. Then read the matching path. The official guide is in the caption.

**Shot progression**

- **0-4s:** Macro tabletop: two paper doors cast long shadows; an adult hand stops between them.
- **4-9s:** Overhead pullback reveals a small handwritten shopping list; persistent FICTION label is visible.
- **9-18s:** Doors unfold into original typography cards labeled Consumer and Distributor; the latter gains an Information checklist.
- **18-25s:** Hand places the purpose note between the cards, then underlines it. No account is chosen or submitted.
- **25-29s:** Hold on the completed decision diagram; small source credit and caption pointer.

**Source:** [Atomy USA registration guide](https://us.atomy.com/cst/userGuide/24100000001691); Consumer steps 1-7; Distributor steps 1-8, particularly step 5. Checked 2026-11-06. The specific supported claim and caption are in the JSON.

**Payoff:** A purpose note turns an abstract account choice into a concrete reading task. **CTA:** The official guide is in the caption.

The FTC recommends comparing product details and the full cost, including delivery and other fees. She unfolds the page. Atomy or another shop: match the item, check the size, then compare the total. The shopping guide is in the caption.

**Shot progression**

- **18-25s:** A hand aligns two unbranded item cards at the same baseline, then points to total cost; neither item is recommended.
- **25-29s:** Final overhead frame preserves the completed checklist and source CTA; no savings figure.

**Source:** [FTC Online Shopping guide](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping); Comparison Shopping; How to comparison shop. Checked 2026-11-06. The specific supported claim and caption are in the JSON.

**Payoff:** The dramatic paper reveal exposes a useful comparison criterion, not a fabricated discount. **CTA:** The shopping guide is in the caption.

## Measurement and release

Keep the experiment ID `opening-01` and the authored variant assignments. Measure each platform/account separately at 72 hours (±12 hours). YouTube’s predeclared primary metric is average percentage viewed; Instagram and Facebook use share rate. Use at least three videos and 1,000 observed views per variant with complete metric coverage before a directional comparison; those are workflow gates, not statistical significance. Use the [growth workflow](SOCIAL_GROWTH.md) for imports, checkpoint matching and replication.

Before production, reread the exact current sources and retain only supported lines. Create the needed original/authorized assets, synthesize and listen to the narration, retime the shot anchors to measured speech, render and inspect the full result, then validate the production package. Only verified packages enter the [publishing queue](SOCIAL_AUTOMATION.md). These briefs do not create account credentials, authorize an advertisement, schedule a post or establish reach.

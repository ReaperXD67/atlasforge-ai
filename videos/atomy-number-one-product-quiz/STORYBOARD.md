## Video direction

Use the BlockFrame preset as a bright editorial quiz show: cream, pink, mint, and electric blue fields; 4px black rules; square cards; 8px hard shadows; Inter for giant answers and Space Grotesk for source chips. The main idea must occupy 40–60% of every portrait frame. Keep all story content in the top 83% and reserve the lower 17% for captions. Nexa is a supporting reaction, never the main subject.

The motion language is VO-paced and decisive. Each frame gets one dominant move, one readable hold, and one small settle. Use direct-to-slot card assembly, hard type swaps, short push-slides, number count-ups, and one-shot stamps. Use `power3.out` for most entrances and `back.out(1.45)` only for the quiz stamp or answer chips. No gradients, lazy breathing, screensaver drift, fake UI interaction, or perpetual bounce. The pattern must change every 2–4 seconds without sacrificing the fact currently being spoken.

Make the sequence loop-aware: the final question hands back to a small “QUICK—” prompt in the opening palette. Use owned Nexa/BizNex assets already in `public/`. Facts are based on Atomy’s official reporting: HemoHIM exceeded KRW 300 billion in global sales revenue; Evening Care approached 5 million sets; Toothpaste+ reached 4.6 million units.

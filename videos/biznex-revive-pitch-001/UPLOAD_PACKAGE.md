## Description

What if a failed recurring payment became an explainable recovery decision—not a lost customer?

Revive is an autonomous revenue-recovery control plane built for the Razorpay AI Buildathon, Track 3. It classifies why a payment failed, recommends the next-best action, applies deterministic customer-trust policies, suppresses duplicate records, and measures incremental recovery with a holdout.

This five-minute product film shows the real hosted decision API, System Proof, Decision Lab, private immutable audit evidence, the duplicate-replay challenge, causal measurement, and the boundaries that still belong on a merchant-pilot roadmap.

Important disclosure: the people, portfolio amounts, recoveries, uplift, and payment outcomes shown are realistic simulated demo data. The hosted API and private audit-store evidence are live. The demonstration does not charge or contact a customer, does not claim merchant production usage, and is not an official Razorpay product or endorsement.

## Video direction

Build one escalating mobile game across seven frames: challenge board, question, answer, harder question, answer, final question, score recap. Keep the BlockFrame candy palette, heavy black borders, hard shadows, giant phone-readable type, and a persistent three-level progress rail. Every question delays the answer and uses a spoken three-count; every reveal advances the score rail. Use smooth long-tail motion, quick push transitions for new levels, and restrained zoom-through reveals for payoffs. Keep all essential content above the lower caption band. Avoid generic card grids, tiny copy, lazy breathing, medical claims, and front-loaded frames that freeze.

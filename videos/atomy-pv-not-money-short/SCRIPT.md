## Frame 1 — PV is a measurement in the compensation plan, not a cash balance.

"A big Atomy P V number is not a big bank balance. P V means Point Value. Atomy's U S agreement defines it as a measurement used in the compensation plan. It is not a dollar balance you can withdraw. Commissions still depend on the plan and your eligibility. So read the label before reading the number. Save this: points measure volume; they do not promise income."

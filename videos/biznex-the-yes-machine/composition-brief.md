# HyperFrames composition brief

Read [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md), [brag-plan.md](brag-plan.md) and [script.txt](script.txt). These are the source of creative intent. Use the measured timing and compiled edit plan as the time source.

- Project: `videos/biznex-the-yes-machine`; final `renders/final.mp4`.
- 1080 × 1920, 30 fps; duration follows measured narration and end hold.
- One continuous, deterministic Three.js miniature; local pinned modules and fonts. No image models or remote runtime requests.
- Camera choreography: macro pressure, reveal, tracking causality, circular match, escalating wide, hard stillness, three physical gates, final choice. Semantic scene modes are authored rather than randomly chosen.
- Text: sparse hook, readable question gates, faithful captions, clear fictional/synthetic disclosure, independent Atomy community context. Never invent a product interface or testimonial.
- Register one paused timeline and keep every visible state a pure function of timeline time. Seek backwards must produce the same pixels. Avoid simultaneous visibility controllers on the same still scene.
- Sound: original score/foley and the checked local voice; compiled speech-driven cues, mastering, encoded-speech validation.
- Run the pinned HyperFrames check with actual text coverage, inspect settled and transition frames, render, decode and review the actual encoded file. A passing source validator alone is insufficient.
- Preserve a strong actual opening frame as the cover rather than splicing in an unrelated flash. Select and export a separate representative poster.
- Do not change the prior film, social queue or publishing schedule.

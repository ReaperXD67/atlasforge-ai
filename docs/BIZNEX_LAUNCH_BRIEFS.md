## Facts versus creative proposals

The first two pairs use the [Atomy USA registration guide](https://us.atomy.com/cst/userGuide/24100000001691), specifically its separate consumer/distributor flows and sponsor-designation steps. The comparison pair uses the [FTC Online Shopping guide](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping), specifically product details and total-cost comparisons. Each factual line has a dated source mapping in the JSON; no actual product price, performance result or customer story is invented.

Mira, Jon, their props and their actions are fictional creative proposals. Every script literally says “A fictional scene.” The planned style is tactile desk cinema: warm side light, paper shadows, restrained macro focus and a purposeful reveal. The paper doors, cup, missing card, ribbons, folded sheet and empty frames should be produced as original assets. There is no claim of copying the reference reel or reproducing its performance.

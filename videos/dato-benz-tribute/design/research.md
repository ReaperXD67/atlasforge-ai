# Benz image-film research

Observed 20 November 2026. This is a focused study for an image-based music film, not a claim to have searched every source or watched complete films. Public creator descriptions and original upload metadata were inspected. No creator footage, music, image or code asset was downloaded into the film.

## Current creative decision

The current brief excludes every photograph or depiction of Benz and all other people. His name appears only in lyrics/dedication. The adopted direction uses 22 person-free main plates (14 retained environments/details and eight new symbols), plus one transparent glass prop. Earlier integrated-portrait experiments and their visual receipts are superseded, excluded local archive material.

## Creative precedents

### Places and materials can carry character

[Patrick Clair's first-person True Detective interview](https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/true-detective/) explains how location expressed character, with landscape photos projected over simple geometry, spatial motion and focus. [Antibody's own Season 3 page](https://antibody.tv/works/true-detective-3/) supplies studio images and credits. The useful lesson here is environmental meaning and depth: a setting can carry the song's emotional subject. No portrait, crime, death, fragmentation or fire imagery is adopted.

[The Peach Kings' Be Around production post](https://www.thepeachkings.com/blog/bearound) credits Paul Trillo and describes a still-photography double-exposure idea developed with landscapes, fabrics and filmed textures. The [band's music page](https://www.thepeachkings.com/music) identifies the official video. This is mixed footage/compositing, not evidence that the original was stills-only. Adapt material continuity across rope, wood, woven shelter, rain glass, roots and water; do not copy multiple exposures or insert people.

### A rejected technique

[Adele's clips page](https://www.adele.com/clips/) links the [original Send My Love video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fk4BbF7B29w). [Director Patrick Daughters' interview](https://shots.net/news/view/90370-adele-s-new-music-video-is-just-gorgeous) describes layered performance takes; interview search text was available but direct fetching timed out. This is source metadata, not a completed frame-by-frame review. Its performance/portrait layering is **not used** in this person-free commission.

The user's earlier golden-light example contributes enveloping light and coherent physical space only. Its person, anatomy and pose are not current film inputs.

## Three concrete methods

1. **Physical symbols with consequences.** Rope fibres share tension; a repair holds; roots protect soil; streams join a river. These communicate loyalty, useful strength and collective care without inventing a documentary act or depicting the honoree.
2. **Varied scale and material handoffs.** Alternate macro, overhead, medium and wide views rather than repeating architecture. Match rope to joinery, droplets to river, table circle to sun and woven fibres to roots. Native close details preserve useful image detail and avoid merely zooming wide plates.
3. **Restrained environment depth and atmosphere.** Reuse the finite-timeline Three renderer for shallow depth, rain/glass/reflection and motivated foreground edges. Keep the frame covered and the lyric region readable. No extra framework is required. The reusable renderer has tested portrait keepout/identity-depth rejection capability, but **no identity asset or keepout region is used in the current film**.

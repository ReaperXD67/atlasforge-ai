# Person-free scene prompts

These eight prompts were executed with the built-in ImageGen tool for the revised Benz film. Each call produced one separate scene; no human photograph or identity reference was supplied. Fourteen previously generated environment/detail plates and one transparent glass prop were retained. Private media, native output paths and execution receipts remain local.

The prompts specify source artwork. Lyrics, camera movement, depth, weather effects and dedication typography are authored separately in the pipeline. Native raster dimensions are recorded locally; 1080p describes the final video output.

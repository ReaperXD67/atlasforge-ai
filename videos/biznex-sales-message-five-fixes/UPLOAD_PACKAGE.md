## Title

Why Nobody Replies to Your Sales Messages — 5 Fixes

## Description

Repair a vague sales pitch in five steps: relevance, a concrete deliverable, honest proof, a small next step, and a respectful follow-up. This BizNex Repair Lab episode works through one fictional café example and gives you a reusable message template.

The five fixes are an original teaching framework, not an experimentally proven conversion formula. We cannot diagnose your actual outreach from a view count or promise that a message will get replies. All examples and businesses are illustrative; no client results or income claims are shown. Replies, clients and income are not guaranteed.

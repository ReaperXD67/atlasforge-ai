# One Card Was Missing From Her Checklist

Deliver a 1080×1920, 30fps, 20–35 second original fictional BizNex story using the approved first-six pilot brief. Rendering was explicitly requested. Use the approved af_heart narration and measured timing.json.

Concept: A fictional adult arranges a self-made checklist; a blank gap leads her back to the official guide.

Payoff: The empty space becomes a specific question to verify, not a recruitment prompt.

Design truth: frame.md. Single continuous prop scene, with five narration-led camera/action phases. The user authorized execution without a creative interview. Source guide remains in the publishing caption; no account selection or submission is depicted.

## Assets
Original CSS paper/ceramic shapes and SVG textures. Locally bundled fonts and GSAP reused from the existing project. Audio and metadata are prepared separately from the exact approved narration. No third-party reel or voice identity is copied.

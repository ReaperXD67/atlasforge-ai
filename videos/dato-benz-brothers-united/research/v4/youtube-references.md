# V4 visual references: authentic photos and cinematic art

Research date: 2026-11-20. Scope: image-based visual storytelling for the 196.760-second Hock Siew tribute, with supplied photographs retaining his real appearance.

## Evidence and provenance

Original YouTube uploads were opened through the documented CUA Chrome browser controls. The two videos below were paused and sampled at four positions each; this was **not full-video playback**. Screenshots were inspected in the tool, but creator media was not downloaded, extracted, or incorporated into the film. Some FLiiiP seeks briefly displayed a buffering indicator, so these observations concern visible composition and colour rather than motion smoothness or exact frame timing.

All adaptations below are our proposed interpretations. They are not claims that the references used our rendering tools or were produced entirely from still images.

## Decisions for this film

1. Keep Hock Siew's actual photo pixels as the identity anchor. Generated environments are poetic art, not fabricated records of his life.
2. Connect three or four visual worlds through one motif, such as a river or shared path. Revisit and expand them with the choruses.
3. Design depth inside each image before animating: foreground framing, readable middle subject, distant atmosphere and useful space for lyrics.
4. Change scale, palette and image density with musical chapters. Alternate intimate and expansive passages instead of repeating one movement.
5. Use restrained occlusion, texture reveals and image relationships. Finish with an authentic personal photograph and a clean Mandarin dedication.
6. Reuse techniques only. Do not copy, rip, trace or redistribute the reference artwork, footage, photographs, music, typography designs or characters.

# Director's specification

**Goal:** leave viewers with one action: ask what they need, what it costs and what they agree to before signing up.

**Output:** HyperFrames, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Integer frame boundaries derived from aligned narration; no guessed timings in the release edit.

**Look:** an ordinary Mumbai apartment. Night photography uses teal window light against amber practical light; the ending uses warm morning sunlight. Visible skin, fabric, rain and architectural detail. Use full-frame photographs, actual generated performance clips when validated, and editable interface inserts. No brown paper tableau or decorative dashboard.

**Type:** Montserrat variable, 700–850, 62–74 px captions; editorial emphasis 112–150 px. Off-white #fff6e9 and amber #ffc97b; dark translucent shadow behind captions. Text stays inside x=90–900 and y=168–1330. One short caption group at a time, maximum four words. Never obscure the eyes or the decisive fingertip.

**Objects:** Mira, smartphone, paused fingertip, rain-covered window, mirror, note interface, notebook, pen. Phone UI is explicitly fictional. Mirror is a photographed physical mirror, not a decorative shape.

**Motion:** direct cuts for hesitation; a 6–10 frame directional bridge from signup to notes; 0.6–1.2 second focus move on the three questions. Photography camera moves are restrained (1.04–1.12 scale, less than 7% travel). Real subject motion is separately identified in the edit plan. Match the final fingertip framing to the opening for a conceptual loop. No constant zoom on every shot.

**Sound:** expressive narration, licensed Scott Buckley score with required credit, contextual ambience and interaction accents. Music ducks under speech and clears the suspended question. Measure the final mix; do not call an unlistened waveform an emotional-quality pass.

The closing line has an additional 6 dB score reduction, eased over six frames before its measured
“Before the next tap” anchor and held through the final pause. This preserves the soft performance
without letting the score compete with its consonants. The narration remains unchanged.

**Shot plan:** authored in EDIT_PLAN.json and resolved against timing.json. Minimum 12 shots, 5 layouts, 4 framings, 3 visual kinds, and 4 different media assets. At least one quiet hold matters as much as the fast cuts.

**Banned:** static paper cards; decorative logo corner; identical scene resets; giant misleading income or health numbers; fake platform testimonials; excessive transitions; boilerplate three-card layouts; subtitles below platform controls; exaggerated camera shake; constant moving text.

**QA:** first frame must show the pending action; inspect every cut midpoint and transition at phone width, face and hand anatomy, subtitle contrast, actual decode, missing assets, frame continuity, final sound peaks and word alignment. Mechanical diversity checks describe the edit; they do not prove audience retention, taste or voice identity.

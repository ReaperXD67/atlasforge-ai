# Visual direction and provenance

These are generation briefs, not claims that a stochastic image model reproduces exact pixels.
The source plates are original generated photographs of the same **fictional adult**. Preserve
the delivered files and hashes to rebuild the exact film. No real person's photograph or voice
was used for the character. All later plates reference the first generated Mira portrait.

## Identity and night portrait

Vertical 9:16 cinematic photograph: fictional Indian woman Mira, 29, medium-brown skin,
shoulder-length dark wavy hair, small mole below the left eye, cream linen shirt. Thoughtful
hesitation while looking down at a phone, in a modest Mumbai apartment at night. Cyan/teal
rain-covered window behind her, amber practical lamp light at left, natural skin and fabric,
50 mm shallow-depth framing, restrained film texture. No logos, text, collage or glamour.
Keep the lower quarter quieter for later editorial captions.

## Wide room

Same woman and night light, a new 35 mm wide composition through a doorway. Mira sits by the
rainy window with a phone in hand. Small ordinary room, foreground doorframe, warm reading lamp,
chair, mug and plant. Show isolation and possibility through physical depth; no luxury cues.

## Phone insert

90 mm macro view of her medium-brown hands and cream sleeve holding a plain black smartphone.
Phone almost front-on, blank dark-charcoal screen for later authored UI. Right index fingertip
pauses above the lower-middle display. Correct natural hands; night window/lamp bokeh behind.
No face, logo or generated lettering. The final button is positioned near the fingertip.

## Mirror

The same woman in a plain wood-framed mirror; her real shoulder and back of head are soft
foreground at the right. Reflected eyes lift toward herself. Lips gently parted, thoughtful
doubt rather than horror or despair. Amber lamp and cool rainy-window spill; no extra person.

## Morning writing

Same identity, shirt and apartment, now peach sunlight and a pale mint wall. Medium side view
at desk height: she writes in an open notebook, phone face down beside it, small genuine-looking
half-smile and relaxed shoulders. Ordinary home, mug, plant, sheer curtain and distant buildings.
Realistic hands and pen, no legible invented notebook text or commercial-success symbolism.

## Morning window

Wide corridor-to-room view. She stands by the open sunny window with a closed notebook by her
side; her phone rests face down on a table. Soft resolved smile, morning light and a breezy
curtain. Keep the ordinary room and recognizable character, no luxury props or triumph pose.

## Morning phone callback

Use the night-phone composition and morning-writing light as references. Preserve phone shape,
hands, sleeve and paused finger position; change the environment to the sunny desk, mug and plant.
Blank charcoal display for editable UI. No rain, blue night light or lamp glow. This makes the
ending's callback happen in the same morning instead of jumping back in time.

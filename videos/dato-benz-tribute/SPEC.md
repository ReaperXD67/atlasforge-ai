# Dato Benz image music film — design specification

GOAL: A living-person tribute told entirely through meaningful, person-free cinematic environments and objects. Benz is named in lyrics/dedication only; no photograph, generated likeness or other depiction appears.

OUTPUT: 1920×1080, 24fps, supplied trimmed recording unchanged. Preserve all 32 measured lyric cues and the retained instrumental/tail. Do not restore the cut introduction. The executable local edit plan defines the exact picture clock and shot count; these are verified after the revised composition is built.

LOOK: Photographic storm coast, wet stone, warm wood, woven fibre, roots, glass and water. Petroleum teal `#102F37`, slate `#35525B`, amber `#D5A15A`, cream `#E4D8BE`, dawn gold `#E6BE79`. Progress from weather and shelter to bright lived-in community, with varied midtones and natural green/blue relief. One physical warm source motivates highlights and reflections. Output resolution is not a claim that source artwork has native 1080p detail; record each native size locally.

ARTWORK: 22 main plates, all person-free: 14 retained environmental/detail plates and eight new symbolic plates (`19`–`26`), plus one transparent glass prop. The eight earlier portrait IDs are excluded from executable inputs and byte-level media bindings. Do not reintroduce them through thumbnails, title backgrounds or closing cards.

STORY: Weathered coast → rope fibres sharing tension → ordinary workshop repair → open community table → roots holding through rain → streams meeting one river → woven shelter → clear dawn harbour. Retained homes, thresholds, glass and reflecting water join these spaces. This is symbolic gratitude, not a claim that a particular photographed charity event happened. Keep the community table welcoming and practical; avoid candles, memorial flowers or a solemn absence motif.

TYPE: Noto Serif CJK SC; sung lines 48px, transparent background, soft dark shadow and continuous lower shade. Minimum 9% horizontal lyric inset and 7% bottom margin. Opening title 58px and closing dedication 45px in genuine negative space. No generated readable text in artwork or opaque caption/name panels. Dato Benz remains exact.

EDIT/MOTION: The song drives the montage. Vary macro, overhead, medium, travelling and wide compositions; use details and larger consequences where the words support them, without enforcing the same pair on every line. Rope → joinery, droplets → river, table circle → sun and woven fibres → roots are available visual handoffs. Retain restrained finite rain/reflection/light, guarded source coverage, shallow environment depth and sparse chapter reveals. Foreground glass remains a physical edge, not a card frame. The final chorus receives a brighter, more open world; the closing settles into the dawn harbour and editorial dedication.

BANNED: Any person, face, body, silhouette, human statue, portrait-like reflection, photo/portrait/card/mount; any Benz or Hock source photograph or identity artwork in the executed film; fake documentary charity events; candles or funeral treatment; all-black/gold voids; uniform zoom/dissolve on every line; repeated near-identical wides; spectrum bars, HUDs and unrelated particles.

QA: Verify the revised plan contains zero identity/portrait assets and that excluded portrait bytes cannot enter the build. Inspect every rendered shot and opening/closing for incidental people, framing, native-detail variety, long-lyric contrast and semantic progression. Test finite deterministic seeking and full frame coverage. After the new master exists, fully decode it, check original-song correspondence and frozen lyric/picture bindings, and inspect actual encoded shot samples. Earlier portrait-version receipts do not validate this replacement. Sampled visual checks remain distinct from continuous playback, listening and client acceptance.

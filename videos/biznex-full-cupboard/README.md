# Her Cart Was Full. So Was the Cupboard.

A standalone fictional Short about noticing unused supplies before adding more. Mira's temptation
is her own decision, not a company purchase requirement. Generic supplies are illustration props;
no actual Atomy pack size, price or product effect is shown.

Use the [batch rebuild guide](../../docs/STORY_BATCH.md), [brief](BRIEF.md) and [design spec](frame.md).
From the repository root, preparation/build accept `--only full-cupboard` for a targeted repair.
The project pins HyperFrames `0.8.174` and the approved female `af_heart` voice.

Verified delivery: **25.6 seconds**, 1080×1920, H.264/AAC, 30 fps. The MP4 passed full decoding,
delivery checks and rendered-ending review; its measured audio is −14.44 LUFS / −1.97 dBTP.
The strict 17-sample audit passes; the closed cupboard intentionally hides its contents before opening.

# Three story routes

1. **The Second Question — selected.** A finger stops above a signup button. The promise of a new start becomes a question about purpose. Night gives way to morning and a written decision. Human hesitation, a visual reversal and a specific useful action.
2. **The Empty Calendar.** An enthusiastic schedule fills itself until a person asks what each hour is for. Strong graphic device, but closer to the previous desk-and-paper films and vulnerable to generic productivity advice.
3. **The Message She Didn't Send.** A draft recruitment message is erased and replaced with an honest question. Strong social setting, but it needs more contextual explanation and could imply invented business results.

The selected route changes the medium, shot scale and emotional arc. The signup interface is fictional editorial illustration, not a reproduction of an official Atomy form.

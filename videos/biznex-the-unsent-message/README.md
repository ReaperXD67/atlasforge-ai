# She Deleted the Perfect Pitch. Here’s What She Sent Instead.

An original fictional landscape film in **The Unsent Message** series. One illustrated evening
connects a crowded cupboard, an overlooked chat and a much shorter reply. The ending is a
conversation, not a product sale.

Use the [batch rebuild guide](../../docs/STORY_BATCH.md), [brief](BRIEF.md) and [design spec](frame.md).
From the repository root, preparation/build accept `--only the-unsent-message` for a targeted repair.
The project pins HyperFrames `0.8.174` and the approved female `af_heart` voice. Chapter markers
reference measured segment IDs, so narration changes retime chapters rather than leaving guessed times.

Verified delivery: **5:03.6**, 1920×1080, H.264/AAC, 30 fps. The final MP4 passed full decoding,
media/voice/chapter checks and review of all 22 scene midpoints plus its opening and ending.
The strict 60-sample audit passes without warnings; closed cupboard doors intentionally hide
contents before opening. Measured final audio is −14.15 LUFS / −1.40 dBTP.

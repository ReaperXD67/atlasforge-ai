## Measurement plan

Record the following at 24 hours and again at 72 hours:

- Shown in feed
- Viewed vs swiped away
- Average percentage viewed
- Average view duration
- Rewatches or loops
- Likes, comments, shares and subscribers gained

Decision rules:

- Average percentage viewed below 65%: shorten setup and move the 30-vs-60 reveal earlier.
- Viewed vs swiped away below 55%: test title variant 1 and a more legible first frame.
- Retention drops at a specific beat: replace that scene pattern in the reusable builder.
- Strong retention but low reach: keep the edit and test a neighboring product-curiosity topic.

No title or edit can guarantee virality. This package is designed as a measurable growth experiment so each upload improves the next one.

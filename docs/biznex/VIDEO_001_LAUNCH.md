# BizNex Video 001 — Launch package

## Strategy

This video tells adults researching Atomy that they can make a calmer, better-informed membership
decision by separating the consumer path, distributor path, official mechanics, and unverifiable
earnings expectations before they register.

The launch uses a high-intent topic as the main story and introduces BizNex as the evidence-first,
AI-assisted channel behind it. A standalone channel trailer was rejected because it gives a new
viewer no search value and spends the opening asking for attention before delivering value.

## Research signals captured on 2026-08-25

YouTube autocomplete clusters current demand around:

- `atomy business plan`, including English and 2025 variants;
- `atomy compensation plan 2026`, `atomy compensation plan usa`, and English variants;
- `atomy products review` and specific product-review searches;
- `atomy usa`, `atomy usa official`, and `atomy usa compensation plan`.

The first video therefore uses the broad search entity `Atomy` plus the current-decision modifier
`in 2026`, while the thumbnail carries the emotional job: pause before committing.

## Chosen concept

**The decision file:** open on the easy mistake—a viewer treats the registration page as one simple
choice—then reveal the consumer and distributor paths, what the official pages actually say, what
they do not establish, and a short pre-registration checklist. Visuals alternate human-tension
documentary footage with owned proof, comparison, and checklist cards. The BizNex AI growth
experiment appears as a brief promise near the close, not as the hook.

Typical directions deliberately left behind:

- a generic “welcome to BizNex” montage;
- a promotional Atomy success story;
- a compensation-plan number dump;
- an adversarial “exposed” video built on unsupported accusations.

## Primary packaging

- **Title:** `Atomy in 2026: What to Know Before You Join`
- **Thumbnail:** `BEFORE YOU JOIN`
- **Hypothesis:** The title captures broad Atomy research intent while the thumbnail adds a
  consequential pause without repeating the query or promising a negative reveal.

### Title alternates

1. `Before Joining Atomy in 2026, Watch This`
2. `Atomy Business Explained: Consumer or Distributor?`

### Thumbnail alternates

1. `KNOW THIS FIRST`
2. `TWO DIFFERENT PATHS`

## Retention spine

| Beat | Final time | Job |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Consumer or distributor? | 0:00–1:08 | Open on the consequential choice, set the verification promise, and establish the two paths. |
| What a sponsor changes | 1:08–2:22 | Explain why the sponsor decision matters and what to verify before designation. |
| Plan vs. typical earnings | 2:22–3:30 | Separate compensation-plan mechanics from typical net earnings and expenses. |
| Five-question checklist | 3:30–4:45 | Give an immediate decision tool, resolve the hook, and introduce the AI growth experiment. |

## Voice direction

Use the local Chatterbox `warm_documentary` character: restrained, conversational, 150–155 effective
words per minute, natural clause pauses, slightly higher intention on the cold open, re-hooks, and
final invitation. If Chatterbox is unavailable, use Kokoro `af_heart` at 0.97 speed and record the
fallback in the manifest.

The launch render used the declared Kokoro fallback because the local Chatterbox runtime was not
available. The final stereo mix measured -15.49 LUFS integrated with -3.16 dB true peak.

## Visual direction

- Warm graphite, ivory, and champagne gold; avoid generic blue/purple AI gradients.
- Four-to-nine-second scene beats with a meaningful pattern interrupt about every 16–18 seconds.
- Real licensed human/action footage for tension and context.
- Owned cards for registration steps, source excerpts, comparisons, and the checklist.
- No money stacks, luxury cars, miracle imagery, fake testimonials, or official-looking Atomy branding.
- Captions remain short, high-contrast, and script-locked.

## Compliance boundary

This is independent educational content. It must not imply endorsement by Atomy, promise income,
present plan payouts as typical net earnings, make health claims, or use a generic disclaimer to
repair a misleading impression. Official/regulatory sources remain linked in the description.

## Post-publish learning plan

Record impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, first-30-second retention, traffic
source, returning viewers, and comments at 24 hours, 72 hours, 7 days, and 28 days. Change one
packaging variable at a time. A low click-through rate with stable retention triggers a title or
thumbnail test; a strong click-through rate with an early retention drop triggers a hook/expectation
review; a mid-video decline triggers a scene-density and proof-order review.

## Pipeline learnings shipped with Video 001

- Lock researched titles, thumbnail copy, description framing, and editorial scripts so a later model
  call cannot silently replace the approved packaging.
- Keep spoken retention promises duration-neutral; final narration timing can change after voice
  selection, pause tuning, and subtitle alignment.
- Reject unsupported Atomy-specific details and effort-to-income implications before rendering.
- Prefer semantic chapter names over auto-generated sentence fragments.
- Use an owned thumbnail background instead of an arbitrary storyboard frame.
- Replace large internal scene numbers on action cards with a neutral check marker.
- Cache or reuse Pexels preview candidates across future runs to reduce repeated search latency; this
  remains the next media-selection optimization.
